SCENARIO 01 ⚡ Zero-Config 1-Second Boarding (Key QR Scan Start) & Garage Auto-Return

Difficulty: Easy · 30 sec

Step-by-Step Actions

  1. On tab 1. Request & Return, in the [Employee Mobile App] panel, select a driver (e.g., Jihoon Kang, Staff) and an idle vehicle (e.g., Vehicle #02 AL 41B-5104 [IDLE]).
  2. Click the button at the very top of the mobile panel: ⚡ 1-Sec Key QR Start (Unit #02).
  3. On the radar map in tab 2. 30-Fleet Telemetry, confirm that Vehicle #02 is moving in blue (AUTHORIZED).
  4. Go to the mobile app's [Request · Return] tab and click the green Auto-Return to HQ Garage (Unit #02) button at the bottom.

Verification Checklist (Expected Results)

  • Does Vehicle #02 switch to normal operation (AUTHORIZED) within 1 second, with no manual Bluetooth pairing or waiting for admin approval?
  • When you click garage auto-return, does the vehicle go back to IDLE and is a trip record (+16.4 km) added to the [IRS Mileage Log] tab?

SCENARIO 02 📋 Standard 4-Stage Dispatch Lifecycle (Request → Approve → In Use → Return)

Core Business Flow · 1 min

Step-by-Step Actions

  1. In the mobile app's [Request · Return] tab, pick an IDLE vehicle (e.g., Vehicle #05 AL 41E-7731), enter the destination (HMMA Montgomery Plant) under 1. Request Vehicle, and click Request (Stage 1: REQUESTED).
  2. On the new request card in the [Dispatch Request · Approval · Active · Return Board], click Approve. (Or click [Approve Now] inside the mobile app for a quick demo.)
  3. On the approved (APPROVED) card, click Start Trip to pick up the key and start using the vehicle.
  4. On the in-use (IN_USE) card, confirm the parking lot (Northpark Lot A) and click Return to return the vehicle and key.

Verification Checklist (Expected Results)

  • Do the 4 status badges (REQUESTED → APPROVED → IN_USE → RETURNED) change in order?
  • Are the request, approval, departure, and return events each logged in the [Recent Notifications] push feed?
  • After a browser refresh (F5), are the return history and tax mileage log still preserved in the backend DB?

SCENARIO 03 🚨 Unauthorized Removal While the Key Manager Is Away (Red Alert) · Co-Passenger Separation · "Depart-first, report-later"

Core Security Scenario · 1 min

Step-by-Step Actions

  1. In the mobile app, select the [Sensor · Alert Test] tab.
  2. Choose an idle vehicle as the target (e.g., Vehicle #06 AL 41F-8890).
  3. With the [With Co-Passenger] checkbox turned on, click the red Unauthorized Start button (start & depart without reporting, 15 km/h+).
  4. After confirming the bilateral Red Alert, click the Submit Reason button that appears next to it.

Verification Checklist (Expected Results)

  • Does the first KPI card (🚨 Unauthorized Trips · RED ALERT) increase by +1 immediately, and does a red alert marker appear on the radar map?
  • Even with 2 people in the car, does the system correctly identify the primary driver using key box BLE (3 m) entry order + driver-seat signal strength (-46 dBm vs -74 dBm)?
  • When you click Submit Reason, does the trip switch to Depart-first, report-later and update to normal operation (AUTHORIZED)?

SCENARIO 04 💥 No-Dashcam Smartphone 6-Axis IMU Crash Reconstruction vs. Mounted-Phone Drop False-Positive Filter

Zero-Dashcam Validation · 1 min

Step-by-Step Actions

  1. Go to the middle section of the mobile app's [Sensor · Alert Test] tab.
  2. First, click the Phone Drop button on the right.
  3. Then click the amber 3.2G Crash button on the left (crash + hard stop).
  4. In tab 2. 30-Fleet Telemetry, check the [Incidents] tab and the 30-second speed-drop graph on the HUD to the right of the radar map.

Verification Checklist (Expected Results)

  • When you click [Phone Drop], is the event automatically filtered out as a false positive because there is no change in driving speed (Δv = 0 km/h) despite the 3.4G impact?
  • When you click [3.2G Crash], does the combination of 3.24G acceleration + 38.4°/s gyro rotation + hard deceleration (-44 km/h) generate an actual collision report?

In the [🚗 30 Vehicles · Odometer Audit] section, enter a number at least 20 km higher than the app's current cumulative mileage in any vehicle's Actual Odometer Audit field (or click with the field left blank to auto-fill +15 km), then click Run Audit.
